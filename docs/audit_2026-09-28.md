# Audit against SIH26042, 28 Sep 2026

Senior-engineer, QA and judge review of the whole repository against R1–R5.
Everything here was checked in this repository on 28 Sep 2026 or is quoted from a
results file named next to it. What could not be run here says so, with the command.

**What could not be run in this audit environment:** the models (about 4 GB, gated
downloads; not in git), the Android build and its JVM tests (Google's SDK host is

latency figures below come from the saved runs made on the team's laptop and tablet.

## 1. Executive summary

**Readiness: 78 / 100.** A real, measured, offline system on a laptop and on an Android
tablet, with unusually rigorous evidence. What holds it back: no real 2 GB device, no
32-bit ARM build, free-form speech only on the laptop, no native-speaker review, and the
demo video not yet recorded.

## 6. Latency and memory

From `bench/results/latency_percentiles.md` (saved runs, recomputed; p50/p90 reproduce the published values):

| Path | n | p50 | p90 | p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tablet, spoken lesson line, Realme Pad Mini (4 GB, Android 11) | 50 | 0.70 s | 1.20 s | 1.27 s |
| Tablet, spoken lesson line, 2 GB Android 9 emulator | 50 | 0.50 s | 0.82 s | 0.93 s |
| Hub, free Hindi speech ≤ 17 words → Santali, from end of speech (3 runs pooled) | 96 | 2.04 s | 2.90 s | 3.29 s |
| Hub, upload to reply audio, Santali → Hindi (per run) | 80 | 2.16–2.21 s | 2.52–2.60 s | 2.67–2.70 s |

Memory (peak PSS): lessons 174–304 MB; spoken lesson lines 655 + 90 MB (2 GB emulator); typed new sentences 1197 MB (2 GB emulator), 1172 MB (Realme).

**Tail mitigations** (not measured here; need the models): translate lines of 12+ words with clause streaming (it gains 0.25–0.29 s to first sound, `README §6`); pre-translate every lesson and every teacher-imported line at sync (already done for lessons); re-run after a 1 s idle pause, the six slowest clips took 3.13 s instead of 4.17 s (median; `bench/results/pause_check.md`): the slow tail follows heavy preceding work, which a classroom pace usually gives time for.
